# Creative style guide (Gen-Z / desi, learned from top Indian agency accounts Arnav shared)
What works right now (cynex.production, cineverze.in, doodhvalefarms examples):
- Hinglish / Hindi hooks with desi humour and relatable pain ("Har business owner ka yahi rona hai", "Scroll mat karna" style reverse psychology)
- Topical hooks: festivals, cricket, trending moments, seasons tied to a business point
- Storytelling carousels where each swipe continues the story (setup → twist → payoff), first slide teases the swipe
- Dialogue formats: client vs agency, chat screenshots, "expectation vs reality"
- Big bold poster typography (Anton / condensed, tape labels, sticker shapes, rotated stickers), pop colours (yellow, red, black) or premium cinematic dark-red
- Metaphor visuals (chess, cricket, cooking, exam) instead of plain text slides
- Clear last-slide CTA: comment a number, DM a keyword, follow
Rules: write original copy (never copy anyone's lines/visuals); no real celebrities, TV/film characters or lookalikes, no other brands' logos; chats/scenarios are illustrative, never call them real client messages; no invented stats.
Mix formats through the week: ~half "Gen-Z pop" (chat/meme/poster) and ~half clean educational (numbered tips) so the feed stays fresh.
